# BenchBook

Simple, open-source record keeping for small plant nurseries.

BenchBook is for the people doing the work. Scan a label, press what happened, and get on with the day. Stock counts, survival figures, plant labels and plant health records come from that, with no separate paperwork.

**Status: planning.** There is no working software yet. The [product specification](docs/spec.md) describes what is being built.

## What it will do

- Record seeds and plants by greenhouse and bench, from sowing to sale.
- Track how many survive, and why some don't.
- Repeat earlier work with one press, using lists instead of typing.
- Print plant labels and bench cards.
- Keep the records needed for UK plant passports and plant health inspections.
- Keep supplier details and simple records of buying and selling.

The first release targets nurseries in England and Wales.

## Technology

ASP.NET Core on .NET 10, PostgreSQL, Svelte 5 and Docker, followed by a native iPhone app.
